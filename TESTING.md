# Testing MAHALA

Build: mechanics-test-2026-10-26

Play without these hints first if you want to check whether the game explains itself. This page gives away the clearing's encounter rules.

## What to try

1. Find the bead sets in the first forest. Check whether you can see and reach them.
2. In the clearing, look for sparkles over four acorns and three berry plants. Try leaving before collecting everything.
3. On separate fresh attempts, try gathering everything without making an offering, feeding the crow three times, or leaving an offering on each stump. Full offerings lead to an encounter after about five seconds of exploration.
4. Try approaching the deer, then continuing to chase them after Mahala's warning. Check that the warning and animal responses make sense.
5. Walk home, enter the last house, cook dinner, bring Mama a beer, wash the dishes and finish the evening upstairs.

Use Start over for a fully fresh test. R retries the clearing, but does not restore a strand that has already broken. The first full offering determines which animal helps. Encounter timers pause during dialogue and scripted interactions.

## Useful feedback

- Which build and operating system were you using?
- Where were you, and what had you just done?
- What happened? What did you expect instead?
- Could you repeat it?
- A screenshot or short recording, if possible.

I am especially interested in missed items, unclear instructions, blocked paths, walking through objects, awkward dialogue placement and confusing encounter triggers. You do not need to write a formal report.

## Current limits

This is an early mechanics build. It ends after the first homecoming sequence. The full story, fox/persimmon opening and drawing system are unfinished. J currently shows clues rather than a complete sketchbook. Art and animation are still in progress.

Linux execution and automated scene checks are being checked locally for this build. Windows and Mac need native testing on those systems; exporting successfully does not verify that they run correctly there. The Windows download is unsigned. The Mac app has an ad-hoc signature and is not Apple-notarized.
